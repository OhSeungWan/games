Clear Grid Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-07

This policy explains how the mobile puzzle app Clear Grid (the "app") handles personal information on iOS and Android.

Summary

  • The app itself does not collect or send personal information. It has no server, no accounts and no analytics or crash reporting.
  • The app shows ads. The ads come from the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which processes the data described below.
  • Once your Remove ads purchase is recognized on your device, the app no longer starts the ad SDK.

What the app does on its own

  • Your progress, records, settings, hint count, ad timing and purchase status are stored only on your device.
  • This data is excluded from iCloud and device backups on iOS, and from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer on Android, so backups do not take it off your device either.
  • Before opening this policy or the terms of use, the app looks up the name ohseungwan.github.io (a DNS lookup) to check the connection. It sends no identifier or content with it. This is the only network request the app makes on its own.
  • This page and the terms of use open in your browser. They contain no scripts, cookies or tracking.

Ads and the ad SDKs

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK, both provided by Google. These SDKs collect and send the following data:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, the Android advertising ID) and the app set ID
  • IP address and the approximate location estimated from it
  • Advertising data and product interaction, such as app launches, taps and ad views
  • Performance data, crash data and other diagnostic data

Google uses this data to show and measure ads, for personalized ads where you have consented, for analytics and to prevent fraud. The UMP SDK uses approximate location, performance and interaction data to decide whether a consent form needs to be shown. The data is encrypted in transit.

The UMP SDK stores your consent choice, and the Google Mobile Ads SDK stores its settings and identifiers, on your device. These are removed when you delete the app. Settings → Reset data does not remove them.

Ad requests are not marked as directed at children, and the maximum ad content rating is G.

For how Google handles this data, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent and tracking

  • Where the law requires consent, the app shows a consent form from Google when it starts.
  • On iOS, after the consent form, the app asks for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission. If you do not allow tracking, the advertising identifier is not available to the ad SDK.

Changing or withdrawing consent

  • In the app: Settings → Ad privacy settings. This item appears only in regions where it is required.
  • iOS: in the device Settings, go to Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn off tracking for the app.
  • Android: in the device Settings, open the Ads settings (under Privacy or Google, depending on the device) to reset or delete your advertising ID.

If you buy Remove ads

Once the app recognizes on your device that you own Remove ads, from then on it skips the consent flow and does not start the ad SDK when it launches. Data sent before that, including after a reinstall until you restore the purchase, is covered by the ad SDK section above.

Payments

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play). The app does not receive your payment information. It only stores on your device whether you own Remove ads.

Ratings and reviews

The app may show the store's rating window. Apple and Google show and process this window. On Android, a rating or review you enter is posted to Google Play. The app does not receive what you enter.

Deleting your data

To delete the data stored by the app, use Settings → Reset data in the app, or delete the app. Data processed by Google is governed by Google's policies above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. The app itself does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page and the effective date above is updated.

Contact

Send questions about this policy through GitHub Issues (https://github.com/ohseungwan/games/issues). Issues are public, so do not include personal information. You can also email face4717@gmail.com.
